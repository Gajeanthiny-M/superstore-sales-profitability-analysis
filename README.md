# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data from a Superstore dataset to identify sales trends, regional performance, and product profitability. The objective was to create an interactive Tableau dashboard that provides actionable business insights and supports data-driven decision-making.

## Business Questions

- Which regions generate the highest sales?
- How have sales changed over time?
- Which product sub-categories are the most profitable?
- Which products are generating losses?
- What is the overall profit margin?

## Key Findings

### West Region Generated the Highest Sales
The West region produced the highest sales revenue, outperforming all other regions.

### Sales Increased Over Time
Monthly sales demonstrated an overall upward trend from 2023 to 2026.

### Copiers and Phones Were the Most Profitable Sub-Categories
These products generated the highest profit and contributed significantly to overall profitability.

### Tables and Bookcases Generated Losses
Despite producing sales, these sub-categories resulted in negative profit.

### Profit Margin Was 12.56%
The business generated approximately $292K in profit on $2.33M in sales.
